## What's New in v2.0.1

- New Shopping List with built-in templates and a home-screen widget for one-tap access
- Month-end cash forecast: scheduled deductions and projected balance at a glance
- Smarter recurring expenses: every-2-months & yearly reminders, with next-charge-date preview
- Manage loan rate adjustments directly; transaction lists now show the year
- Split bills now log only your own share
- Tap any day in the daily-spending chart to see details

Various bug fixes, faster loading on Notes and Loan pages, plus English & Japanese support.
