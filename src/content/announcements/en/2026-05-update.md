This update brings a set of practical and delightful new features to make tracking and daily life feel closer to home.

## Highlights

- **Too shy to reach out?** Use our built-in "Good Morning Cards" to share warmth with family and friends on WhatsApp, Messenger, or Instagram—just like a wholesome Grandma would!
- **Tired of manual entry?** Scan QR codes or receipts with your camera to finish bookkeeping in a snap. We even support image recognition!
- **Made a mistake in your entries?** No need to start over! You can now easily edit lists on the POS page. Keep your accounts tidy and stress-free.
- **Always forgetting expiry dates?** Exclusive for Premium Members: Use "Expiry Date Scanning" to track freshness with just one tap.
- **Interested in the Lunar Calendar?** We've added "Sexagenary Cycle" (Stems-and-Branches) info! Master traditional wisdom right in your hand.
- **Smoother than ever:** We've fine-tuned the UX details for a more fluid tapping and swiping experience.
