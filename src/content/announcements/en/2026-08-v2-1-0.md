This update graduates the Living Room Finance Channels into the one and only home for your financial overview!

## What's New

- **Living Room Finance Channels, fully live**: New Loan & Investment Pairing, Property Valuation, and Leverage Health (debt payment ratio) channels join the lineup, officially replacing the old stats page — this is now the only place to check your financial overview.
- **"Program Guide" renamed to "Replay"**: Channels are reorganized by topic, so finding the one you want is just a flip away.
- **Smarter loan splitting**: Sharing a loan with family or friends? Now you only need to record "your share" — principal and interest are calculated automatically, keeping everyone's books clean.
- **Better note search**: Searching notes now also surfaces related calendar events and reminders, so you don't have to look in multiple places.
- **Refreshed home & to-do cards**: A cleaner Light Mode with a solid background, a flip-clock countdown animation for upcoming items, and custom category ordering.
- **Added support for Czech Koruna (CZK) and Australian Dollar (AUD)**
- Several stability improvements for a smoother experience overall.
